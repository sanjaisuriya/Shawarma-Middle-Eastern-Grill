Our Heritage & Roots

From Ancient Spice Markets to the Open Hearth

What began in 1998 as a family spice and grilling stall in the historic market lanes of Aleppo has evolved into a welcoming home for authentic Middle Eastern comfort dining.

Founded by Master Pitmaster Tariq Mansour and his family, our passion has always centered around the sacred connection between fire, meat, and mortar-ground botanicals. When our family relocated, we brought our hand-beaten brass mortar, heirloom sourdough starter for fresh saj bread, and seven-spice family recipe across continents.

Today, Shawarma & Middle Eastern Grill stands as a tribute to those generations of culinary craftsmanship. Every dish we serve is rooted in reverence for Levant traditions—never taking shortcuts with pre-ground factory seasonings, liquid smoke, or artificial marinades.

"Authentic Levantine cooking isn't fast food—it's an ancient art of patiently balancing citrus acidity, garlic warmth, and slow-searing hardwood fire."

— Chef Tariq Mansour, Founder

Our Culinary Process

The Art of Vertical Roasting & Flame Seared Skewers

Creating the perfect shawarma requires hours of calculated patience and exact culinary execution.

1. 24-Hour Citrus & Spice Infusion

Fresh hand-sliced chicken breast and ribeye cuts are marinated for a full 24 hours in extra virgin olive oil, cold-pressed lemon juice, garlic paste, cardamom, nutmeg, and smoked Aleppo pepper.

2. Stacked Cones on Vertical Rotisseries

Our master pitmasters tightly layer marinated slices into dense vertical cones weighing up to 40 kg, alternating lean muscle with thin cuts of lamb tallow for continuous self-basting richness.

3. Shaved to Order & Blistered on Saj Flatbread

Only the crisp outer caramelized layer is shaved with a sharp curved blade upon ordering, wrapped into freshly flipped dome saj bread, and toasted on the plancha with a brush of spiced marinade.
