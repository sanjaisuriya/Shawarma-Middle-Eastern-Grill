❓ Answers to Common Questions

Catering Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers covering serving size estimates, transparent pricing, dietary accommodations, and order changes.

Our packages are formulated with generous Middle Eastern hospitality portions. Each adult portion includes approximately 6–8 oz of seasoned protein, along with hearty scoops of saffron rice, fresh salad, warm pita, and dips. For big eaters or longer events, we recommend adding an extra meat tray or meze platter.

Our package prices serve as transparent starting points. You are welcome to swap proteins (e.g., all chicken or adding lamb kofta), substitute salads, or add dessert and drink stations. Our coordinator provides an itemized proposal reflecting your exact selections with zero hidden fees.

All poultry, beef, and lamb are 100% certified Halal. For vegetarian and vegan guests, we prepare dedicated falafel, hummus, baba ghanoush, and fattoush platters using dedicated utensils. Gluten-free guests can enjoy all rice bowls, grilled meats, and salads without bread crisps.

Guest counts and menu tweaks can be updated up to 48 hours prior to delivery for standard buffet orders. For large banquets requiring live pitmaster setups, final details are locked 4 days prior. Cancellations made with at least 72 hours notice receive a full deposit refund.
