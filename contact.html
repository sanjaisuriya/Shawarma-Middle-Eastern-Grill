Getting Here & Parking Information

🚗
Driving Directions

From Highway 101, take Exit 24B (Downtown/Arts District). Head east along Grand Avenue for 1.2 miles, then turn right onto Spice Market Way. Our restaurant is located at the corner of Cedar Avenue.

🅿️
Complimentary Customer Parking Lot

We offer a private, well-lit 40-space customer parking lot directly behind the restaurant. Enter easily through Spice Alley at the rear.

🚘
Weekend Valet Parking

Complimentary valet service is available for dine-in guests every Friday and Saturday evening from 6:00 PM to 11:30 PM.

🚇
Public Transit & Metro Access

Central Metro Station (Gold & Blue Lines) is just a 4-minute walk (0.2 miles). Bus lines #14, #28, and #42 stop directly at the Spice Market Plaza crosswalk.

♿
ADA Accessibility & Special Needs

Level zero-step main entrance with automatic doors, wide aisles, fully accessible restrooms, and 4 dedicated accessible parking bays adjacent to the front doors.
