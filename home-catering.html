Frequently Asked Catering Questions

Everything you need to know about lead times, delivery coverage, and order modifications.

For standard buffet trays, platter boxes, and Corporate Lunch packages (up to 30 guests), we recommend placing orders at least 24 to 48 hours in advance. For large banquets exceeding 50 guests, weddings, or live pitmaster barbecue stations, we ask for at least 5 to 7 days advance notice to ensure dedicated staff and equipment allocation.

We deliver within a 30-mile radius of our central kitchen. All buffet and platter orders come packed in heavy-duty insulated thermal containers. For orders over ₹3,000 / $300, we include wire chafing stands, water pans, sterno fuel canisters, serving tongs, and heavy-duty compostable plates and cutlery at no additional charge.

We follow generous Middle Eastern hospitality standards. Our formulas allocate 250–300 grams of cooked protein per adult guest, complemented by 200 grams of seasoned rice or batata harra, 2 fresh saj flatbread sheets, and generous allocations of mezze dips and salads. We guarantee your buffet line will never run empty before all guests are fed.

Yes, we understand guest lists fluctuate. You may adjust your headcount, swap side dishes, or add extra vegetarian options up to 48 hours prior to your scheduled event time. Our Catering Director will adjust your invoice and confirm updated quantities via email and phone.
