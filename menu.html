Levant Culinary Tradition

The Four Pillars of Our Kitchen

Authentic Middle Eastern cuisine is an alchemy of slow time, radiant hearth fires, and pure ancestral seasonings. We reject shortcuts, artificial tenderizers, and pre-packaged doughs—every creation follows the uncompromising craft of Levantine master chefs.

🥖

Convex Dome Saj Bread

Each flatbread is hand-stretched paper-thin and blistered across our authentic 450°F convex steel dome in seconds. The result is a delicate, flaky wrap that crisps on the grill without tearing, sealing in garlic toum and savory juices.

🥩

24-Hour Spiced Marinade

Our prime chicken thighs, lamb flanks, and beef chuck soak for 24 hours in extra virgin olive oil, freshly crushed garlic, wild Syrian sumac, Turkish Aleppo pepper flakes, and aromatic Lebanese seven-spice (Baharat).

🪵

Live Hardwood Oak Hearth

We burn 100% natural oak and citrus-wood lump charcoal reaching 600°F. The intense radiant heat creates our signature caramelized outer crust while locking inside supreme juiciness and subtle aromatic woodsmoke.

🧄

Dawn-Whipped Silk Meze

Our cold meze are prepared fresh every sunrise: heirloom chickpeas simmered until tender and whipped with roasted sesame tahini, thick mountain-strained labneh with za'atar, and our signature emulsified garlic toum.

The Royal Banquet Experience

Authentic Flavors, Shared at Every Table

In Middle Eastern culture, dining is synonymous with Karam (unconditional generosity and warmth). Meals are meant to be a feast of colors, aromas, and textures passed across the table with hot bread and fresh herbs.
