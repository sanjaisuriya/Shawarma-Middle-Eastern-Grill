📋 Service Guarantees

Ordering Guidelines & Policies

Key details regarding our Halal options, spice customizations, transport logistics, and lead-time schedules.

🌱
Dietary Preferences

Halal options available. Clearly labeled vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free items prepared with dedicated utensils to prevent cross-contact.

🌶️
Custom Spice Levels

Select Mild (warm Levant aromatics), Medium (Aleppo pepper warmth), or Spicy (red shatta chili kick). We also supply sauce pots on the side.

🚗
Delivery & Pickup Options

Free kitchen pickup in temperature-insulated boxes. Direct event delivery available up to 25 miles with optional setup assistance.

📅
Advance Booking Lead Time

Express boxes require 24 hours notice. Large family feasts and live grill pitmaster stations require 48 to 72 hours advance confirmation.

Pricing & Catering FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about guest adjustments, payments, dietary needs, and delivery radius.

The rates displayed on this page are sample demonstration prices. Final quotes are tailored depending on your exact headcount, custom protein substitutions, delivery distance, and requested on-site services. When you submit an enquiry, our team provides an itemized written quote before any deposit is charged.

Yes! You can increase or decrease your estimated guest count up to 48 hours before the event date for standard buffet packages. For large banquets involving live pitmaster setups, headcount finalization is requested 4 days in advance to secure fresh meat and staffing.

All poultry, beef, and lamb served across all our packages are certified 100% Halal. We offer dedicated vegetarian and vegan trays (such as Herb Falafel, Creamy Hummus, Baba Ghanoush, Tabouleh, and Fattoush) packed and labeled separately to ensure complete dietary safety.

We deliver within a 25-mile radius of our Foodville kitchen. Food is transported in heavy-duty commercial thermal carriers maintaining safe temperatures (above 140°F for hot roasted meats, below 40°F for meze and dressings). Setup assistance and buffet staging are included for all orders over ₹350.

Buffet packages include disposable wire chafing racks, water pans, and 2-hour heating sternos, along with serving tongs, heavy-duty disposable plates, cutlery packs, and napkins. If you prefer elegant ceramic platters or full live chef grilling, we offer upgraded service tiers upon request.
